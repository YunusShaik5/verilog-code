A QUICK FIELD GUIDE
From code to “I get it.”
01
Describe your circuit
Paste your module in design.v, or choose an example from the sidebar. You can include multiple modules in the same file.
02
Give it something to do
The testbench.v file changes inputs over time. Click Generate testbench for a starter that detects ports, common clock names, and reset names. Review the generated stimulus to match your design.
03
Run, play, explore
Click Run simulation, then ▶ to animate. Click a waveform or drag the timeline to inspect a moment. The arrows step through signal changes.
What the pictures mean
The diagram comes from synthesis; the waveforms come from your testbench. Green is 1, gray is 0, amber is X, and blue is Z. Timing is simulated, not a measurement of a physical chip. Optimized or unmapped internal wires stay gray.
Standard Verilog and supported SystemVerilog syntax run in Icarus. Only synthesizable design code can produce a diagram. A diagram error does not prevent simulation. Runs have a time limit and can be stopped.
